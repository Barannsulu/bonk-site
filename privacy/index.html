Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

BONK collects nothing. Everything stays on your phone.

BONK is a private app for adults (18+) that keeps a passport of the countries the people you've been with were from.

What we collect

Nothing. BONK has no account, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer receives no data from the app, and the app makes no network requests.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Your entries. Each entry is one person, recorded only as the country they were from (for the United States, optionally a state) and the time you added it. Entries made with older versions of the app may also contain fields you entered then (a category, a rough date, a city, a private note).
  • Your settings, including the optional "Where are you from?" country and the optional passport name.

This data stays in the app's private storage on your phone. Your phone's own backup (for example iCloud) may include it, depending on your settings.

What we never ask for

Names, photos, contact details or locations of anyone, and no GPS location.

Sharing

BONK only creates an image (a "share card") when you ask for it and confirm what it shows. It goes to your phone's share sheet; where it goes next is up to you. Numbers and your passport name are hidden unless you switch them on. US states and dates are never shown.

Export and restore

"Export my data" creates a file with your entries and settings and hands it to your phone's share sheet. Anyone you give the file to can read it. "Restore from a backup" reads a file you pick and only imports countries, US states, times and entry ids.

Face ID

If you turn on App lock, iOS checks your Face ID or passcode. BONK never sees your face data.

Deleting your data

Settings → "Hold to delete all my data" erases every entry and setting. Deleting the app also removes its data from the phone.

Children

BONK is only for adults 18 and older.

Built-in data

Country shapes from Natural Earth (public domain); places and populations from GeoNames (CC BY 4.0); country names from Unicode CLDR. They are bundled in the app; no requests are made to these services.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
