Questions

Is it a dating app?

No. There are no other users, no messages and no matching. BONK is your own private log.

Who can see my passport?

Only you. A share card leaves your phone only when you make one and choose where to send it.

What if I get a new phone?

Export your data from Settings, then restore it on the new phone.
